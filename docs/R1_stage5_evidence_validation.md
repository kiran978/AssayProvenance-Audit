## Interpretation limits

- Shared depositor and shared grant establish administrative/project-related links, but **neither demonstrates measurement duplication nor rules out independent assay execution**.
- A direct AID xref or explicit AID mention may document a sequential or orthogonal experimental stage; it does not establish identical experimental measurements.
- The manuscript's prior 0/43 "confirmed independent" result is still a **historical finding conditional on its old definition**, not newly validated by this extraction.
- The 43 records comprise a selected diagnostic cohort, not a population-level prevalence sample.
- The old `NONINDEPENDENT_SAME_DISCOVERY_PROGRAM` labels remain visible in the **non-blinded** machine-evidence output for audit traceability, but are omitted from the **blinded** human-review form.

## Next scientific acceptance gate

Obtain at least two qualified **human** expert reviewers to assess primary evidence **blindly** for all 43 pairs. Require a documented source URL / exact source location, discussion of copied data vs separate execution vs separate discovery program, original reviewer signatures/dates or equivalent traceable decision records, reconciliation of disagreements, and unresolved/hold category where affirmative evidence is insufficient. No reviewer agreement statistic should be reported before those real decisions exist.
